how an edit comes together 🌿

1. Start with the light

Exposure and contrast set the foundation. Think of it like choosing what time of day your photo was taken.

2. Shape the shadows

Highlights and shadows let you rescue details in the bright sky or dark corners. Pull shadows up to reveal hidden textures.

3. Find the color story

Temperature shifts warm ↔ cool. Tint adds green or magenta. Saturation and vibrance control how loud the colors speak.

4. Add texture & mood

Clarity sharpens or softens details. Vignette darkens the edges to draw the eye inward. Grain adds that analog film feeling.

5. Make it yours

Stickers, text overlays, rotation, and blend modes are the finishing touches. There are no rules here — just your vision.
