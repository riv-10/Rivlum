our privacy oath

This isn't a legal document written by lawyers in a tall building. It's a promise from one creator to another — written in plain language because you deserve to know exactly what happens (and doesn't happen) with your stuff.

🌿 How we keep RIVLUM soft & safe

  • 1
    Your photos never leave your device Every edit happens right inside your browser using your phone or computer's own processor. When you upload a photo, it goes into your browser's memory — not to any server, not to any cloud, nowhere. It's yours and it stays yours.
  • 2
    No cookies, no trackers, no analytics We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, Hotjar, or any other tracking tool. We don't set cookies. We don't know how many people visit, which buttons you click, or how long you stay. And honestly? We're totally fine with that.
  • 3
    No accounts, no emails, no personal info There is no signup form. There is no login screen. We don't ask for your name, your age, your email, your phone number, or anything else. You just open the site and start creating. That's it.
  • 4
    The only outside connections are fonts & sample photos To make the site look nice, we load two fonts from Google Fonts. To give you something to edit right away, we pull a few sample images from Unsplash. Those are the only two outside services RIVLUM talks to — ever.

📦 What lives in your browser

RIVLUM uses something called localStorage — a tiny pocket of memory inside your web browser. Think of it like a sticky note your browser keeps for you. Here's exactly what we save there:

Key What it stores
rivlum_theme Your chosen color theme (meadow, ocean, sunset, or midnight)
rivlum_dys Whether you turned on the OpenDyslexic font
rivlum_motion Whether you turned on reduced motion
rivlum_stars Which challenges you've completed (just a list of names like "prism")
rivlum_drifts Your saved edits — slider values, stickers, and a copy of the image
rivlum_born The date you made your very first edit (so we can show your "days creating" stat)
rivlum_tips Little tips you've saved while editing
rivlum_restore A temporary copy of your last free edit so you can pick up where you left off

Want to delete everything? Just clear your browser's site data or use the "Clear All" button on the homepage. Poof — gone forever. We have no backup because we never had a copy. 🧹

👩‍👧 For parents & teachers

If you're a grown-up checking whether this site is safe for the young person in your life — hi, thank you for caring, and here's the short version:

✅

There is no backend server. RIVLUM is a collection of static HTML files. There is no database, no user accounts, and no way for us to collect, store, or access any data from any visitor.

✅

There is no social interaction. The "co-edit room" feature works by encoding edit settings into a shareable URL link. There is no chat, no messaging, no comments, and no way for strangers to contact your child. They can only share a link with someone they already know.

✅

There is no advertising or monetization. No ads, no affiliate links, no sponsored content, no "upgrade to premium" upsells. RIVLUM is and will remain completely free.

✅

There is no data collection of any kind. No cookies, no analytics, no fingerprinting, no third-party scripts. The site is fully COPPA-compliant by design because there is literally nothing to comply with — we collect zero data.

✅

All content is age-appropriate. The sample photos are landscapes and nature scenes from Unsplash. The language is warm and encouraging. The challenges teach real photo-editing concepts (exposure, contrast, color temperature) in a gentle, gamified way.

If you have any questions or concerns, this site was built by a 16-year-old creator who genuinely cares about making a safe space for young artists. The entire codebase is simple enough to inspect yourself — just right-click and "View Page Source" on any page. What you see is what you get. 💛
