🛡️ Privacy Policy (COPPA Compliant)

Last Updated: October 2024

1. Zero Data Collection

RIVLUM does not collect, request, or store any personal user information. There are no registration forms, email requirements, or user profiles.

2. Local Storage

All photos, edits, custom "Drift" recipes, unlocked challenge stars, and accessibility preferences stay 100% local to your device using your browser's internal localStorage. No image data is ever uploaded to external servers.

3. Hosting & Infrastructure

RIVLUM is hosted securely on Render's static delivery network. Render delivers the website files to your browser without tracking personal identity, collecting cookies, or running analytics scripts.

4. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA)

RIVLUM fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, children can safely explore and edit photos without privacy risks.
