Your Privacy

the shortest privacy policy you'll ever read

the tl;dr 📝

RIVLUM collects nothing. No name, no email, no photos, no cookies, no tracking, no analytics. Your edits live on YOUR device. When you close the tab, they stay with you. We literally cannot see anything you do here.

📵 what we DON'T collect

  • Your name, age, email, or any personal info
  • Your photos or videos (they never leave your device)
  • Your location, IP address, or device info
  • Cookies for tracking (we don't use any)
  • Analytics or usage data (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel)
  • Ad tracking (there are no ads on RIVLUM)

💾 what stays on YOUR device

RIVLUM uses your browser's built-in storage (called "localStorage") to remember things like:

  • Which challenges you've completed (so we can light your stars)
  • Your "Drift Gallery" (edits you saved)
  • Your theme choice (Meadow / Ocean / Sunset / Midnight)
  • Any tips you wrote to Pixel

All of this stays ONLY on your device. It never gets sent anywhere. If you clear your browser data, it's all gone — no backup, no recovery, no records. That's the point.

📸 your photos

When you upload a photo to edit, it's processed entirely inside your browser. It NEVER gets uploaded to any server. When you hit "Save," the edited photo downloads directly to your device. RIVLUM has no server that stores photos — it doesn't even have a server. Just static files on Netlify's CDN.

🌐 external stuff we use

To keep RIVLUM working, we use a few third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — loads our fonts (Outfit, Caveat). Google may log the font request per their policy.
  • Unsplash — the challenge photos come from Unsplash's free photo library.
  • Netlify — hosts the website. May log basic server requests (like any website).

We can't control what these companies log on their end. But RIVLUM itself sends nothing.

👶 for parents

RIVLUM is designed with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) in mind. Because we don't collect ANY personal information, we're not required to get parental consent — but we welcome parents to explore the site with their kids. There's nothing to sign up for, nothing to worry about.

If you have questions, email hello.rivlum@gmail.com.

✏️ changes to this policy

If RIVLUM ever changes what data it handles (which is currently: none), we'll update this page and add a note at the top. Because we don't collect emails, we can't notify you directly — so please check back if you're curious.
