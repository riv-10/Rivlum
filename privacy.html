Privacy & Safety Oath

built for trust, agency, and creative freedom 🌊

RIVLUM was engineered from day one to protect young creators. We believe software should serve curiosity without treating children as data points to be monetized.

🌿 The Core Pillars

🔒

Zero Account Registration

No passwords, no email addresses, no phone numbers. Anyone can land on RIVLUM and start editing instantly with zero friction.

📸

Local Device Processing

Your photos never touch a remote cloud server. All pixel manipulations run directly inside your browser via the client-side HTML5 Canvas API.

💾

Browser LocalStorage Only

Saved color recipes, unlocked challenge stars, custom "Drifts," and accessibility preferences remain 100% on your own device.

🌐

Clean, Static Infrastructure

RIVLUM is delivered securely across Render's global static distribution network. Render serves the pure website files without running analytics trackers, cookies, or targeted advertising scripts.

📜 Plain-Language COPPA Commitment

In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), RIVLUM does not collect, solicit, store, or sell any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or any age).

If a user clears their browser cache or local storage, all saved progress and custom recipes are permanently wiped on their device. We hold no backup copies because we never collected them in the first place.

💡 For Parents & Teachers

Q: Can RIVLUM be used safely in classrooms?
Yes. Because RIVLUM requires no login credentials, collects zero data, and contains no chat rooms or public feeds, it is completely safe for classroom assignments and art labs.

Q: How do shareable URL links work?
Portfolio and Co-Edit Room links encode lightweight color values and text directly into the link address itself (`#portfolio=...`). No image or user data is ever sent to or stored on an external database.
