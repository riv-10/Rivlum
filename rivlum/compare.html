What happened here?

Look at the trees. In the raw version they look dark and heavy. The editor added warmth (sepia) to make the sunset glow wrap around the branches, reduced contrast to reveal misty details, and gave the shadows an orange splash.

🧅 The Edit Layers

Every great edit is just a stack of small decisions. Here's the recipe:

1Brightness +15% — lifted the overall light
2Contrast -10% — softened the shadows
3Saturation +35% — made colors pop
4Warmth +25% — added golden sunset glow
