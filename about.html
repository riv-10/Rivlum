Our Little Promise

We built this space with one very simple rule: your creativity belongs to you, and your privacy is not for sale.

  • 📸 Everything you edit stays on your device — we never see your photos, not even for a second
  • 🚫 No signups, no emails, no passwords, no tracking pixels, no "we value your privacy" popups (because we just… do it)
  • 📢 No ads, no sponsored content, no "recommended for you" algorithms deciding what you should see
  • 🧹 Your saved edits live in your browser's localStorage — clear your browser data and it's all gone, just like that
  • 🗄️ We don't even have a database. There is literally nothing to leak. The server is just a folder of HTML files

the story

🌊 What is RIVLUM?

RIVLUM is a free, open photo-editing playground for kids and teens (ages 8–16). Think of it as a gentle first step into the world of color grading, light editing, and visual storytelling.

It runs entirely in your browser — no downloads, no installs, no servers storing your work. Every edit you make uses your device's own processing power, and every saved "drift" lives in a tiny pocket of browser storage called localStorage.

The name comes from the idea of a river of light — how light flows through a photograph and how you can gently redirect it to tell your own story. 🌊✨

🛠️

the tools

🛠️ How It Works

✨ Challenges: Try to match a target edit by adjusting 10 real photo-editing sliders — exposure, contrast, temperature, grain, and more. Our Drift Engine compares your result and tells you how close you got.

📸 Free Edit: No targets, no pressure. Just you, a photo, and all the sliders. Save your drift to your portfolio whenever the mood strikes.

⚡ Co-Edit Rooms: Share a link with a friend and take turns editing the same photo. Everything happens through the URL — no servers, no accounts, no chat.

🌊 Portfolio: Your saved drifts live in a little gallery you can share with anyone via a link. It's your creative diary, and it's entirely yours.

💻

under the hood

💻 Built With Love (and HTML)

Plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no build tools, no npm packages, no React, no Tailwind. Just the web, the way it was meant to be — fast, simple, and accessible on a cheap phone over a slow 3G connection.

The only external resources we load are two Google Fonts (Atkinson Hyperlegible for readability and Caveat for that handwritten feel) and some sample photos from Unsplash so you have something beautiful to edit right away.
