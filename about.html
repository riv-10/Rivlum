about RIVLUM

the river of light

RIVLUM started because I wanted to learn photo editing but couldn't find a single app that was free, private, and didn't overwhelm me with complicated tools.

So I built one. A cozy little corner of the internet where kids can experiment with light, color, and creativity — no sign-ups, no ads, no judgment.

RIVLUM is my love letter to every kid who wants to create but hasn't found the right tools yet.

— Ritvija, founder 🇮🇳

🤝 our promises to you

  • We will never collect your data. Not your name. Not your photos.
  • We will never charge kids. RIVLUM is free forever.
  • We will never show you ads.
  • RIVLUM works offline. No internet? Still works.
  • We will never fake-praise you. Honest feedback only.
  • No mistakes in editing — only drifts.
