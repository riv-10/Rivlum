🌊 What is RIVLUM?

RIVLUM is a free, open photo-editing playground for kids and teens (ages 8–16). Think of it as a gentle first step into the world of color grading, light editing, and visual storytelling.

It runs entirely in your browser — no downloads, no installs, no servers storing your work. Every edit you make uses your device's own processing power, and every saved "drift" lives in a tiny pocket of browser storage called localStorage.

The name comes from the idea of a river of light — how light flows through a photograph and how you can gently redirect it to tell your own story. 🌊✨

🛠️ How It Works

Challenges: Try to match a target edit by adjusting 10 real photo-editing sliders. Our Drift Engine compares your result and tells you how close you got.

Free Edit: No targets, no pressure. Just you, a photo, and all the sliders. Save your drift to your portfolio whenever you want.

Co-Edit Rooms: Share a link with a friend and take turns editing the same photo. Everything happens through the URL — no servers involved.

Portfolio: Your saved drifts live in a little gallery you can share with anyone via a link.

💻 Built With

Plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no build tools, no npm packages. Just the web, the way it was meant to be — fast, simple, and accessible on a cheap phone over a slow connection.

The only external resources we load are two Google Fonts (Atkinson Hyperlegible for readability and Caveat for that handwritten feel) and some sample photos from Unsplash so you have something to edit right away.
